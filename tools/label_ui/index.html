QA flags

    Keys and mouse

    Everywhere: drag empty space = pan · pinch or ctrl/alt+scroll = zoom · two-finger scroll = pan · F fit player · 0 whole image · +/- zoom

    Clip events: ←/→ one frame · shift = 10 frames · Space play/pause · C contact = this frame · B max backswing = this frame · K show/hide model skeleton · Enter go to keypoints

    Keypoints: drag a point to move it · Tab/shift+Tab select next/previous point · click an unplaced point's row, then click the image to place it · B selects the ball · shift+click moves the selected point · V visible · O occluded (estimated) · U unknown (removes it) · R reset to model · ⌘Z undo · ←/→ (shift = 10), Space or the scrubber: look at other frames, with your points drawn faint · / or Enter back to the labelled frame · H hide labels · G model ghosts · N/P next/previous frame · Enter confirm frame · E back to events
