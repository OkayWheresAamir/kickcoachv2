Feedback rating sheet

What you're rating. Each item is the feedback for one problem the app found in one kick. Some were written by an LLM and some are fixed templates; you aren't told which. Beside each one are the facts the writer was given and the text of every source it cites.

Correctness: check each sentence against the facts and the cited sources. If the trunk number says "−14, negative = leaning back" and the text says you leaned forward, that's a 1. A claim nothing beside it supports (e.g. "this costs you power" with no source) counts against it, even if it sounds like normal coaching advice.

Specificity: could this text be pasted onto someone else's kick? Using this kick's number and what it means = high; general football advice = low.

Actionability: imagine you're the player. Could you do the cue and set up the drill at your next session, alone, without asking anyone what it means?

Rate what's written, not how you'd write it; use 2 and 4 freely. Your clicks are remembered in this browser, so you can close the file and come back. When you've finished (or want to hand over what you have), press Download ratings and tell Claude where the file went.
